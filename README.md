One image that sums up the world's news of Thursday, 26 November 2026: a
newspaper front page — "The World in One Day" — with 36 dated stories across
8 regions. The hero is Ukraine's strike on Savasleyka airbase (3 MiG-31s and
2 Su-34s damaged); seven illustrated stories follow (the Cairo tanker fire
that killed at least 31, the Houthi claim on Saudi Arabia's biggest refinery,
Asia's stock rally, the Israeli court's signal on Netanyahu's bribery charge,
Puerto Rico voiding the Luma power contract, US tariffs on 14 partners, and
the EU summit's €1.78T budget), plus a pin map, a UTC timeline, a markets
strip and a 28-item "more today" list. Every story is dated 26 November 2026.
The image is drawn from a hand-written HTML page (`build/page.html`, rendered
to `entry.png` with headless Chromium); the program just copies it to
`/output/entry.png` after checking it is a whole PNG inside the limits.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
